QuantCompass Web

Guided, academically sound statistics for students, in your browser.

In development. The validated statistical engine is ready; the browser interface is the next stage. Until then, use QuantCompass Local (runs with R on your computer).

When finished, you will upload a CSV or Excel file, describe your hypotheses, and get valid tests, thesis-ready results and the exact R script that produced them. Your data stays in your browser.
